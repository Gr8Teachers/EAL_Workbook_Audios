Show transcript – Sorting money

Look at the coins and notes on the table. They are different. Can you name each one? 

Yes. There is a 10-cent coin, a one-dollar coin, a 5-dollar note, a 10-dollar note and a 20-dollar note.  

Great. Now put them in order from low to high.  

Oh. The 10-cent is low.

Good.  

Then one dollar, then 5 dollars, then 10 dollars, then 20 dollars. 

Well done.
