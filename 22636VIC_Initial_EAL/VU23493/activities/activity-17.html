Adding, symbols and related words

Adding is when you put 2 numbers together.

In everyday tasks, you use addition when you buy more than one item. You also add to pay with more than one note or coin.

Symbols

You use the plus symbol + when adding numbers.

You use the equals symbol = to know the total.

Review the examples:

Sometimes, you can also use a long line instead of the equals sign. Review the examples in the table.

Show transcript – In the classroom

Teacher: I have 3 boxes of pens. Look at this box. How many pens in this box?

Student: 5.

Teacher: Look at this box. How many pens in this box?

Student: 10.

Teacher: Look in this box. How many pens in this box?

Student: 20.

Teacher: Does this box with 20 pens have more pens than this one?

Student: Yes, this box has 20 pens. That box has 10 pens.

Teacher: Does this box with 10 pens have more pens than this one?

Student: Yes, this box has 10 pens. That box has 5 pens.

Teacher: Now, put them in order. From low to high.

Student: The box with 5 pens goes here, then the box with 10, then the box with 20.

Teacher: Correct. Well done.
