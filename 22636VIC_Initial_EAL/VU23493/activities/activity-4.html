Show transcript – Digits in numbers

Look at the table in the workbook. Listen to the information. 

Single-digit numbers

Every number has digits. 

The numbers from 1 to 9 have one digit. They are called single-digit numbers.

The single-digit numbers are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9. 

A single-digit number is also called a unit. 

Double-digit numbers 

Numbers 10 and above have 2 digits. They are called double-digit numbers. 

The numbers from 10 to 19 are 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19.

The numbers between 13 and 19 are called the 'teens'.
