Show transcript – Meeting your new teacher

Listen and answer the questions.

Teacher: Hi, how are you? I’m Nicola. I’m your teacher. 

Student: Hi, nice to meet you. 

Teacher: Um… What’s your name?  

Student: It’s Jian.  

Teacher: Nice to meet you Jian. So first, can I ask you some questions? 

Student: Yes, OK.     

Teacher: What’s your full name? 

Student: It’s Mr Jian Chen. 

Teacher: Can you spell your name, please?  

Student: My given name is J-I-A-N. My family name is Chen. That’s C-H-E-N. 

Teacher: OK, thanks. And what days do you come to class? 

Student: Sorry?  

Teacher: What days do you come to class? 

Student: On Thursday and Friday. 

Teacher: OK, great. Next, what’s your address?  

Student: It’s 189 Alphabet Street, Bexley.

Teacher: Pardon? What’s the street name?

Student: It’s Alphabet Street.

Teacher: OK, thank you. Now, your seat... Do you want to sit at the front or the back? 

Student: At the front, please. 

Teacher: OK, great. That’s all. You can sit here. 

Student: Mmm... Thank you.
