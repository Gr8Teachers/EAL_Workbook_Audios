Show transcript – Meeting a neighbour

Listen and answer the questions.

Neighbour 1: Hi. I’m Mei. I live in Unit 10. 

Neighbour 2: Hello Mei. My name’s Dina. 

Neighbour 1: Nice to meet you. Where do you come from? 

Neighbour 2: I’m from Egypt. I came here in January. 

Neighbour 1: Oh, that’s nice. I came here in March.  

Neighbour 2: Sorry. Could you repeat that, please? 

Neighbour 1: I came here in March. 

Neighbour 2: OK… Where are you from? 

Neighbour 1: From China.  

Neighbour 1: OK, nice... Do you like living here? 

Neighbour 1: Yeah, it’s great. I like Australia. Do you have family in Australia?

Neighbour 2: Um, I have 2 kids. Um... Who lives with you?  

Neighbour 1: My husband and my kids.  

Neighbour 2: Oh, that’s great. How old are the kids? 

Neighbour 1: They are 10 and 8 years old. Um... Sorry, I have to go now. 

Neighbour 2: OK, bye. Nice talking to you.

Neighbour 1: OK, bye.
