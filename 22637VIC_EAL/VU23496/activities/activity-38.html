Show transcript – Talking to a neighbour

Listen and answer the questions.

Dina: Hi Mei. How are you? 

Mei: Hi Dina. I’m good, thanks. How are you? 

Dina: Fine, thank you. Um… Can I borrow some sugar, please? I don’t have any sugar…and I want to make a cake.

Mei: Pardon, can you say that again? 

Dina: Sorry. Can I borrow some sugar, please? 

Mei: Sure, come in. 

Dina: Thanks.

Mei: Do you want a cuppa? 

Dina: Sorry. Can you say that again, please? 

Mei: Do you want a drink?  

Dina: Yes, please. 

Mei: Do you like tea or coffee? 

Dina: Coffee, please. Yes, um, thanks. Er, it’s my son’s birthday today.  

Mei: How old? 

Dina: 11 years old. I want to make a cake for his birthday.

Mei: Oh, yes. OK, sure. I understand… Here’s some sugar…and here’s a coffee.  

Dina: Oh, thanks.
