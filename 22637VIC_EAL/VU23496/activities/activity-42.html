Show transcript – Talking to TAFE reception staff

Listen and answer the questions.

Staff: Good afternoon. How can I help you? 

Student: Hi. I want to change my days, please.

Staff: When do you have lessons now?

Student: On Thursday and Friday.

Staff: What days do you want to come?

Student: On Monday and Tuesday, please. What time?

Staff: Lessons start at 9 am.

Student: What time do I finish?

Staff: At 1:30 pm.

Staff: OK. I have a few questions.

Student: OK.

Staff: Now, what is your full name?

Student: It's Jian Chen.

Staff: What is your classroom number?

Student: Room 12.

Staff: And... Do you like your class?

Student: Yes, it's easy. I like it. Um, er... I work on Friday.

Staff: OK, I understand. You can come on Monday and Tuesday. Room 9.

Student: What is the teacher's name?

Staff: It's Holly.

Student: OK, thank you. Bye.
