Show transcript – Practice script - Sunny day

Teacher: Hello.

Student: Hi.

Teacher: Let’s talk about the weather.

Student: Yes, OK.

Teacher: It’s sunny today. It’s warm. It’s 23 degrees. Is it a nice day?

Student: Yes, it’s a nice day. It’s sunny. It’s warm. It’s 23 degrees.

Teacher: It’s going to rain later.

Student: Sorry, can you repeat that please?

Teacher: It’s going to rain later.

Student: That’s no good.

Teacher: That’s all for today. See you later.

Student: OK. Bye.
