A question is a sentence. You ask a question to:

  • listen again

  • understand better

  • learn more.

Speaking partner A Speaking partner B
How are you going? I’m well thanks, how about you?
How’s your sister? She’s good. She has a new job at a bank.

To ask a question, the sentence starts with a question word.

Question word Example
Who Who is that?
What What is your name?
Where Where are you from?
Why Why are you sad?
How How are you?

Examples of questions in a conversation are:

Speaking partner A Speaking partner B
Where are you from? I am from China.
How do you spell that? C-H-I-N-A
