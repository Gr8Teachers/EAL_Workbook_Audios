You use the word ‘can’ to ask someone to do something. For example:

Picture Sentence

Can you tell me the time?

Can you hear me?

The question ‘Can you repeat, please?’ asks someone to say what they said once again.

Speaking partner A Speaking partner B
What time of the day is it? It is morning.
Can you repeat that, please? Yes, it is morning.

Ask for help

If you don’t understand, you might ask questions to help you. For example:

  • Sorry, I don’t understand.

  • Can you speak slowly, please?

  • Can you say that again, please?

  • Can you help me, please?

  • Can you spell that, please?

  • What is that?

  • Do you mean this?

Look at the table for examples of how to use these questions in a conversation.
