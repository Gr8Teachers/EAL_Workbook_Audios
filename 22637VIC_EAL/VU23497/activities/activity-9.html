Show transcript – Practice script 3 - Cold day

Teacher: Hello.

Student: Hi.

Teacher: Let’s talk about the weather.

Student: Yes, OK.

Teacher: It’s cold today. It’s cloudy. It’s only 10 degrees. Is it a nice day?

Student: No. It’s not a nice day. It’s cold. It’s cloudy. It’s only 10 degrees.

Teacher: It’s going to rain.

Student: Sorry, can you repeat that please?

Teacher: It’s going to rain.

Student: That’s no good.

Teacher: That’s all for today. See you later.

Student: OK. Bye.
