Conversation 1 

A: Did you have a good weekend? 

B: Oh yeah. I visited my friend and his family on Sunday. I helped him fix his car, then we had lunch together. In the afternoon, we went to the park and played soccer with his children. 

Conversation 2 

A: Did you go out on the weekend? 

B: No, I just stayed at home. On Saturday, I cleaned the house and cooked a cake. On Sunday, I helped my son with his homework and then we watched a DVD and went to bed early. 

Conversation 3 

A: How was your weekend? 

B: Not bad. On Saturday afternoon, I went shopping for a new mobile phone and on Saturday night, I went to the movies with some friends. On Sunday, I spent the day at the hairdresser’s. She braided my hair. 

A: It looks great. 

B: Thanks.
