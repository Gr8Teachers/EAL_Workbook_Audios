Show transcript – Track 3

Conversation 1 

Customer: Excuse me, have you got Splitoff headache tablets? 

Shop assistant: Yes, they’re over here. Now there’s the packet of 24 or the bottle with a hundred. 

Customer: I’ll take the packet thanks. 

Shop assistant: The packet? Sure. That’ll be $5.95. Thank you. 

Customer: Thanks. Bye. 

Conversation 2

Shop assistant: Hi. How can I help you? 

Customer: A hamburger, please. 

Shop assistant: Sure. Anything else? 

Customer: Orange juice? 

Shop assistant: Over there. 

Customer: Oh, OK. 

Shop assistant: So just the hamburger and the orange juice? 

Customer: And these, thanks. 

Shop assistant: Two packets of chips. Right, that’s um… $8.75 altogether. [fade]

Conversation 3

Shop assistant: Can I help you? 

Customer: Could I have a whole snapper, please? 

Shop assistant: What size would you like? 

Customer: Medium, about one kilo. Mmm, yes that one, that one looks good. How much is it? 

Shop assistant: Let’s see. That’s $17.30. 

Customer: That’s fine. 

Shop assistant: Anything else? 

Customer: No. That’s all. Thanks. [fade] 

Conversation 4 

Shop assistant: Are you next? 

Customer: Yes. Can I have 6 of those bread rolls, please? 

Shop assistant: These ones here? 

Customer: Yes, thanks. 

Shop assistant: Anything else for you? 

Customer: That’s all. Thanks. 

Shop assistant: That’s $2.40. [fade]
