Grammar: Present simple tense

We use present simple to talk about actions happening now. We use different verb endings and forms as shown in the table.

Grammar Examples
Present simple

I go shopping.

I like television.

She eats cake.

We go to the park.
Verb ‘to be’

I am the teacher.

He is a student.

They are students.
Verb ‘to have’

I have a car.

We have a house.

He has a dog.

She has a phone.
Verbs (action verbs)

We run to school.

The children play.

I throw the ball.

I talk to my friend.

The bird can fly.

I like to swim.

Contractions

We make 2 words shorter with an apostrophe. This is a contraction.

For example:

Positive Positive contractions
I am a teacher. I’m a teacher.
You are married. You’re married.
He is a student too. He’s a student too.
She is a widow. She’s a widow.
It is big. It’s big.
We are from Russia. We’re from Russia.
They are from Chile. They’re from Chile.

Here are examples of negative contractions:

Negative Negative contractions
I am not from China. I’m not from China.
You are not from China. You’re not from China.
He is not from China. He’s not from China.
She is not from China. She’s not from China.
It is not from China. It’s not from China.
We are not from China. We’re not from China.
They are not from China. They’re not from China.
