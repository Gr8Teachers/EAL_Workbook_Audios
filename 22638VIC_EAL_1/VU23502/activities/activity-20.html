Listen to At the medical centre – Dr Jones.

Talk with your speaking partner. Say the exchange. Take turns to be the receptionist and the patient.

This is the transcript of the audio.

Receptionist: Good morning. Can I help you?

Patient: I’d like to see Dr Jones please.

Receptionist: Have you been here before?

Patient: Yes, I have.

Receptionist: What’s your surname?

Patient: Smith.

Receptionist: And your first name?

Patient: John.

Receptionist: Right. Have you got your Medicare card there?

Patient: Yes. Here it is.

Receptionist: Okay. You can take a seat.

Patient: How long will the doctor be?

Receptionist: He’s quite busy, possibly an hour.

Patient: Right. Thanks.
