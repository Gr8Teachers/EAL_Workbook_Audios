Instructions 2

Here is some vocabulary you will need before you listen.

Picture Words Meaning

Announcement

Spoken information to tell you something. It usually has an explanation. It sometimes includes instructions.

An announcement is usually made through a speaker.

Train You can catch a train to go somewhere.
Figure 101 Train platform Where you stand to get on a train.

Yellow line on platform This is the line on a platform. You stand behind this line.

Customer Someone who pays for a thing or service. A person who catches a train is a customer.
