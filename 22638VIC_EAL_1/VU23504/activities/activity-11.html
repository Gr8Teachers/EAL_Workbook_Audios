Pre-listening: Vocabulary

Before you read an advertisement you need to learn some vocabulary you might find in an advertisement for a place to rent.

Picture Word Description

Bedroom A room to sleep in. It has a bed and sometimes a wardrobe.

Garden An area outside a place. It can be at the front or the back of the place.

Dining room A room to eat meals in.

Kitchen A room to cook meals in.

Lock up garage or garage A place to park a car inside.

Laundry A room to wash and dry your clothes.

Inspect To look at a property so you can decide if you want to rent it.

Application form A form to fill-in to apply to rent a property.
