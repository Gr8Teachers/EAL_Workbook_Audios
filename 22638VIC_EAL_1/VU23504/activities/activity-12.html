Read information: Advertisement

You will read 2 advertisements for places to rent.

Scenario:

Rashaad and his family have decided they would like to move to Paramatta. They decide to read advertisements about places to rent. They need to decide on the type of place they want.

They want to rent a place that:

  • has 3 bedrooms

  • has a laundry

  • has a lock up garage

  • has a garden

  • is near a park

  • is near a train station or a bus stop

  • is near cafes and restaurants.

Rashaad will read both ads so they can decide where to move his family.

Show transcript – Looking for a flat

Narrator: Unit 5. Housing. 5A. Looking for a flat.

Speaker 1: Good morning! How can I help you?

Speaker 2: Oh, hello. I'm looking for a two-bedroom flat in this area. Oh, with a garden if possible. 

Speaker 1: Right, let me see. We've got two at the moment. There's one in Darling Street. 

Speaker 2: Right.

Speaker 1: That's got two bedrooms, dining room, kitchen and a good sized living area.

Speaker 2: Ooh.

Speaker 1: It also has a lock-up garage and a small garden. That's 365 per week.

Speaker 2: And the other one?

Speaker 1: Right, well that's a two bedroom townhouse with a garage, separate dining room, kitchen, living room and a reasonable garden.

Speaker 2: Ooh, where's that one? It sounds good.

Speaker 1: That's in Wellington Street. And its only 325 per week.

Speaker 2: Does it have a laundry?

Speaker 1: Yeah, it does.

Speaker 2: Okay, well, I'm interested in looking at both of them.

Speaker 1: Okay, you can inspect them this Saturday between 12 and 3pm. Here are the addresses. Just go along, have a look. If you're interested, pick up an application form and bring it back here!

Speaker 2: Terrific! Thanks, I'll have a look and get back to you.

Speaker 1: Okie doke. Bye.
