Show transcript – Describing cities

Description 1:

There are lots of things to see and do in and near Perth. Perth is a modern city with many parks and gardens. It's the capital city of Western Australia, and it's located on the Swan River. Freemantle and Rottnest Island are near Perth, and they are popular places for tourists to visit. Freemantle has lovely old buildings, markets and Italian restaurants. Beautiful Rottnest Island is 30 mins by ferry from Freemantle. It is famous for its clean beaches, birds and other interesting animals.

 

Description 2:

Surfer's Paradise is in Queensland. It's on the Gold Coast, southeast of Brisbane. It's a very popular holiday area, so there are lots of shops, restaurants, bars and hotels. There are long white beaches, so many people go swimming and surfing there. Children like it because it is near a lot of theme parks such as Movie World and Sea World.

 

Description 3:

Well, there are lots of things to see and do in Canberra, and it's a very popular place with tourists. Parliament House is an interesting place to visit, and the National Gallery of Australia has many wonderful paintings. Canberra has wide streets and lots of trees, parks and gardens. There is a big lake in the middle of Canberra called Lake Burly Griffin. People walk and cycle around the lake. There are also ferry cruises on the lake.
