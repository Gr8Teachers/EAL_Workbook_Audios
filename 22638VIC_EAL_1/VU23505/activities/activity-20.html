Activity 21

  1. Choose a place that you like. You can write about one of these topics or about another place or thing:

  • A place I like to visit: A description of a favourite place to visit, such as a park or shopping mall, river or beach. Write about its features (for example, BBQ areas, playgrounds) Write about why you like going there (for example, seeing local wildlife).

  • A day at the beach or rive: A description of a favourite place to visit, such as a river or beach. Write about its features (for example, picnic areas, swimming spots) Write about why you like going there (for example, swimming, playing in the sand or having a picnic).

  • An Australian birthday party: A description of a birthday party in Australia. Write about the decorations or games. Write about the food (for example, fairy bread or sausage rolls).

  • Weather and seasons in NSW: A description of the different weather and seasons in NSW. Write about what might happen during summer, autumn, winter and spring (for example, heatwaves, drought or floods). Write about what you like to do in the different seasons.

  1. Plan your descriptive text using the table. Remember to use dot points and short sentences. Check your answers with your teacher.

Student 2:

Ana-maria comes from Chile. She is a widow. She was a nurse in Chile. She lives in Lyndholme with her family: 2 sons and 1 daughter. She comes to class because she wants to be a nurse in Australia.

 

Student 3:

Garang comes from Sudan and he speaks Dinka. He is 21 years old. He is single, and lives in Yannan with 3 friends. Garang's family doesn't live in Australia. He comes to class because he wants to be a mechanic.

 

Student 4:

Leila comes from Turkey and has been in Australia for 6 months. She lives in Banksmead with her husband. Leila was an accountant in her country. She comes to class because she wants to work as an accountant in Australia.
