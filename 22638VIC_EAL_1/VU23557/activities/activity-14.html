Interview – playing soccer 

Speaker A: Hi, can I ask what you do in your free time?  

Speaker B:  Sure. 

Speaker A: Thanks. So, what is your favourite leisure activity? 

Speaker B: Easy, I like playing soccer. 

Speaker A: Great, can you tell me about soccer?  

Speaker B:   Yes, soccer is a team sport. Your team works together to kick the ball into the other team’s net. This is a goal. The team with the most goals wins.  

Speaker A:  Where do you play soccer?  

Speaker B:  You play soccer outdoors on a sports field. 

Speaker A: How do you use the ball?          

Speaker B:  Good question. You can use your feet to kick the ball. You can use your body to stop the ball, but you can’t use your hands.  

Speaker A: Gee, that sounds hard. What do you need to play soccer?  

Speaker B: You need soccer boots and shin guards to protect your legs.  

Speaker A: So, why did you take up soccer? 

Speaker B: I wanted to join in after watching my daughter play soccer. It keeps me fit, and it is a lot of fun. Now, my daughter comes to my games to cheer on my team. I like that.
