Show transcript – Leisure activity conversations

Conversation 1

Speaking partner A: Can you cook?

Speaking partner B: Yes, I am a good cook.

Speaking partner A: What do you enjoy cooking?

Speaking partner B: I like trying different recipes.

Conversation 2

Speaking partner A: Can you drive?

Speaking partner B: Yes, I can drive a car and a truck.

Speaking partner A: Oh, really?

Conversation 3

Speaking partner A: Do you want to play a game of tennis?

Speaking partner B: Sorry, I can’t play tennis.

Speaking partner A: I could teach you.

Speaking partner B: OK, as long as you are patient.

Conversation 4

Speaking partner A: Can you swim? 

Speaking partner B: A little. I’m taking swimming lessons on Saturday afternoons. 

Speaking partner A: Oh why? 

Speaking partner B: I want to be a better swimmer.  

Conversation 5

Speaking partner A: Do you like dancing? 

Speaking partner B: I love it! 

Speaking partner A: Would you like to come dancing with me? 

Speaking partner B: Sure. When? 

Speaking partner A: I go every Tuesday night.  

Conversation 6

Speaking partner A: I need two more people for a soccer team. Can you play Soccer? 

Speaking partner B: Yes. I can.

Speaking partner A: Would you like to join the team? 

Speaking partner B: Sure. 

Conversation 7

Speaking partner A: Wow, look at that guy on the surfboard. 

Speaker B: Yeah, he’s great. I’d like to learn to ride a surfboard.  

Speaking partner A: Yeah, me too.

Speaking partner B: Why don’t we take surfing lessons? They have them at this beach. 

Speaking partner A: Yeah, why not? 

Conversation 8

Speaking partner A: Can you ride a horse?

Speaking partner B: I don't know. I've never tried. What about you?

Speaking partner A: I went horse riding once, but I didn't like it.

Speaking partner B: Oh.
