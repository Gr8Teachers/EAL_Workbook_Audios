What, where, who why questions

Here are examples of ‘wh’ questions.

What Where Who Why
We use what to ask about things. We use where to ask about places. We use who to ask questions about people. We use why to ask for reasons.
What are you cooking? Where is my recipe book? Who is my teacher? Why do you like cooking?
What do you want to eat? Where are you going? Who is the cook? Why is the bus late?
What are you doing? Where is the car park? Who can help me? Why is soccer practice cancelled?

Conversation – cooking

Person 1:  What leisure activity do you like? 

Person 2: I like cooking. 

Person 1: What do you cook? 

Person 2: I cook different foods from my country. 

Person 1: Where do you cook?  

Person 2: I cook inside in a kitchen. 

Person 1: Who do you do cook for? 

Person 2: I cook for my family and friends. 

Person 1: Why do you like it?  

Person 2: It’s relaxing. I feel happy.
