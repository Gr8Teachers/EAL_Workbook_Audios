Conversation – bike riding

Person 1: What do you like doing in your free time? 

Person 2: I like bike riding. 

Person 1: Where do you ride? 

Person 2: I ride outside. 

Person 1: What do you need? 

Person 2: I need a bike and a helmet. 

Person 1: How do you ride? 

Person 2: I sit on a bike, and I use the pedals to move. 

Person 1: Why do you like it? 

Person 2: I like it because it’s fun.
