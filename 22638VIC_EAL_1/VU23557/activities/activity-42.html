Conversation – soccer

Speaker A: What do you like doing?

Speaker B: I like playing soccer.

Speaker A: Who do you play with?

Speaker B: I play with friends in a team.

Speaker A: What do you need?

Speaker B: I need a soccer ball, soccer boots and shin guards.

Speaker A: How do you play?

Speaker B: You kick the ball into the other team's net. This is a goal. The team with the most goals wins.

Speaker A: Where do you play?

Speaker B: I play outside. I play on a sports field.

Speaker A: Why do you like it?

Speaker B: I like it because it keeps me fit.
