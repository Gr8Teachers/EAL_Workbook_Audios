Speaking partner A: What do you like doing in your free time?

Speaking partner B: Well, I'm a student. I am studying childcare at TAFE, so I don't get a lot of free time. But when I do, I like shopping and playing sport. I really like playing tennis and soccer. In summer, I love swimming and riding my surfboard. What about you, what do you like doing in your spare time?

Speaking partner A: I don't really like playing sport. I like reading and listening to music, I also like watching TV, and going to the movies. I don't mind swimming in summer, but that's about the only exercise I like doing.
