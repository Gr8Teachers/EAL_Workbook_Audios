Show transcript – Rayna and Sam

Rayna: Hi Sam. 

Sam: Hi Rayna. How’re you doing? 

Rayna: I’m good. How was your weekend? 

Sam: Oh, it was great.  

Rayna: Really? What did you do?  

Sam: My family and I went to the markets on Sunday. We met my sister and her children there because we wanted to do some shopping together.  

Rayna: Nice. Which market did you go to? 

Sam: We went to Paddy’s Markets. It’s not far from our house.  

Rayna: Oh, it’s nice there. I always go to the markets. What was the weather like?  

Sam: Um, it was a warm day but there was a nice breeze. We were very lucky.  

Rayna: Very lucky. It was very hot where I live. Did your kids have a good time? 

Sam: Oh, yeah! They enjoyed walking around and looking at the stalls. They loved the toy stall. They had a wonderful time. What about you? What did you do? 

Rayna: Oh, I just stayed at home and relaxed. I watched movies and did some work around my house. I also went out for dinner with some friends. It was good to see them.   

Sam: Fantastic. I think my class is starting now. I should go. 

Rayna: Yes, my class is about to start too. I’ll talk to you later.  

Sam: Bye.
