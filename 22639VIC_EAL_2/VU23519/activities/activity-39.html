Katrina: Hi Boris. Another Monday at work again. How was your weekend?

Katrina: What did you watch on Netflix? 

Boris: I watched a movie called Stay Close. 

Katrina: How was it? 

Boris: It was great. It was a murder mystery. 

Katrina: Oh really! I love watching murder mysteries. 

Boris: Yes, me too. How about you, what did you get up to on the weekend? 

Katrina: Well, I went to Manly beach with my friends. 

Boris: Oh, Manly beach. I love Manly Beach. What did you do there? 

Katrina: We went for a swim, but it was a little cold. After that we had lunch at an Italian

restaurant. 

Boris: I love Italian food. How was your lunch? 

Katrina: It was delicious.

Boris: And after lunch, what did you do?

Boris: Sounds great. Much more interesting than my weekend. 

Katrina: I don’t know. That Netflix movie sounds interesting.

Boris: Gosh is that the time? I better go. I’ve got computer training with David from I.T.
