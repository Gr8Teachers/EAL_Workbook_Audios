Show transcript – Invitation

John: Hello, John speaking.

Sarah: Hi John, it’s Sarah, how are you doing?  

John: Great, what about you? 

Sarah: Not too bad, pretty busy at work and with the family. Hey John, just want to let you know that Tim and I are having a barbecue on Sunday and we were hoping that you and Zoe could come. 

John: Oh, that sounds great, but is your barbecue this Sunday?  

Sarah: Yes, that’s right. We want to have a few people over while the weather is still warm.   

John: Oh, I am so sorry Sarah. Zoe and I would love to come, but it’s my mum’s birthday and we have made plans to take her out this Sunday. 

Sarah: No worries, that’s absolutely fine. We can catch up another time. Where are you taking your mum? 

John: Well it’s a big surprise, but we are going to take her to see a show and then out to dinner. 

Sarah: Sounds great. Say a big happy birthday from Tim and I. 

John: Sure will. Speak to you soon. Bye. 

Sarah: Bye.
