Show transcript – At a BBQ - meeting someone new

Alex: Hi! I’m Alex. Beautiful day, isn’t it?  

Sam: Yes the weather is perfect. Are you one of Sarah’s friends?  

Sam: Yes, I am! Nice to meet you, Alex. I’m Sam. How do you know Sarah? 

Alex: We used to work together.  

Sam: Oh great. What do you do?  

Alex: I work in hospitality. We were both working on the front desk at Hotel Sydney in the city. And you, how do you know Sarah? 

Sam: I know Sarah from yoga class. A group of us usually get together after class on Saturdays and have a coffee.  

Alex: Ah that’s so nice! 

Sam: Do you live around here, Alex? 

Alex: Mmm not really. I live across the bridge but it’s not too far away ....I love hanging out with Sarah, so I often pop over for a chat. So, do you work Sam?  

Sam: Yeah, actually I’m a teacher at the local high school. It certainly keeps me busy! 

Alex: Wow, I bet it does! How’s the work-life balance? 

Sam: Mmm, not so good to be honest because there's always so much preparation and marking to do. Anyway, it's good to get out on the weekends and just relax. Hey, ...have you tried the lamb kebabs? They are Sarah’s special recipe. 

Alex: Really! They smell amazing! I better grab one before they all go. 

Sam: Uhm, I’m just going to get a drink. Would you like one?  

Alex: I’m ok at the moment thanks. Nice to meet you, Sam. 

Sam: You too, Alex. See you around!
