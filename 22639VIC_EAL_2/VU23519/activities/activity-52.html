Making requests politely

It is important to make requests politely. If we are polite, then people are more likely to do what we want. To be polite we use words like could and would.

Would it be possible for you to come early in the morning?

Could you come and have a look at my hot water system?

Show transcript – Calling a plumber

Peter: Emergency Plumbing Services. 

Tony: Could I speak to Peter White, please? 

Peter: Speaking. 

Tony: Peter, my name is Tony Hamal. I'm a tenant in a property at Northcote. Ellen from Rocket Real Estate told me to phone. 

Peter: Oh, yeah. 

Tony: My hot water heater is broken. Do you think you could come and have a look at it? We really need to have the hot water on. 

Peter: Well, I can't come today. I'm full up today. 

Tony: Well, when could you come? 

Peter: What about tomorrow? 

Tony: Ok. Any chance you could come first thing in the morning? There'd be someone here then. 

Peter: Yeah, um, I could come at uh, 8 o'clock? How's that sound? 

Tony: Yeah, that'd be great. 

Peter: So what's your address? 

Tony: 13 Catherine St, Northcote. 

Peter: Ok, ah, just give us your phone number just in case something comes up. 

Tony: Ok. It's 9478 6738. 

Peter: 6738. Got it. I'll see you at 8 o'clock tomorrow morning. 

Tony: Righto. See you then. 

Peter: Bye. 

Tony: Bye.
