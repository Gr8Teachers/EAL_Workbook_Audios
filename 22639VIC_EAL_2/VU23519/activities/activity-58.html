Show transcript – Shopping for a washing machine

Assistant: Good morning sir. How can I help you? 

Customer: Hi, I'm looking for a washing machine. 

Assistant: Do you know what size you want? 

Customer: Um, probably medium. We don't have a lot of room in the laundry. 

Assistant: Mm, what about a 5 or 5 point 5 kilo? We've got them over here if you'd like to take a look. 

Customer: Sure. 

Assistant: We've got Simpson, Whirlpool and Fisher and Paykel. All these machines have been discounted. 

Customer: I'm interested in a Fisher and Paykel. 

Assistant: Ok. There are two Fisher and Paykel models. There's this basic 5 kilo model, and this 5 point 5 which has more cycles - delicates, permanent press, regular and heavy duty. 

Customer: Mm, and how long is the warranty? 

Assistant: Two years. 

Customer: I think I'm interested in the 5 point 5. Now, is that the best price you can give me? I've seen the same machine 40 dollars cheaper at another store. 

Assistant: Oh. Well, what are they charging for delivery? 

Customer: Thirty dollars. 

Assistant: Well, I can match their price and give you free delivery. 

Customer: Okay, when could you deliver it? 

Assistant: Tomorrow. 

Customer: Great. Can I pay by credit card? 

Assistant: Sure. 

Customer: Okay, I'll take it.
