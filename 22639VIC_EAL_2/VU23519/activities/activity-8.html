Show transcript – Talking to your neighbour

Sofia: Hi Rosie. How’s it going? 

Rosie: Not bad. How about you? How’s the family? 

Sofia: We’re fine, thanks, but a bit tired today. Did you hear that car alarm last night? 

Rosie: Yes. Wasn’t it terrible? It kept us awake all night. It went on for hours. 

Sofia: Yes, it was just awful. So loud! Next time I’m going to ring the police! 

Rosie: I hope it doesn’t happen again. Carlos needs to get some sleep tonight. He’s got a job interview tomorrow. 

Sofia: Oh really? What’s the job? 

Rosie: It’s a sales job with a computer company.  

Sofia: That’s great. Where is it? 

Rosie: It’s in Penrith. 

Sofia: Oh, that’s handy. Well wish him luck for me.  

Rosie: Thanks. I will. 

Sofia: Oh, that reminds me. Are you and Carlos free on Saturday night? We’re having people over for drinks. 

Rosie: Yes, we’ll be around. That’d be great. What time? 

Sofia: Oh, around 5. 

Rosie: Terrific. We’d love to come. But I’ll talk to you before then. I’d better run. I’ll be late for the train. 

Sofia: Ok. See you later.  

Rosie: Yes. See you later. Bye.
