Show transcript – Tasmania 1

Hi everybody, today I'm going to tell you a bit about Tasmania. It's sometimes called the apple isle because it's famous for its apples. But many Australians prefer to call it Tassie. Okay, so this is Tasmania down here. As you can see, it's an island, and it's separated from the mainland by Bass Strait. So if you want to go to Tasmania, you have to fly or take a ferry. The name Tasmania comes from the first European to see the island, a Dutchman called Abel Tasman, who sailed down the west coast in 1642. Now, Tasmania's not very big compared to the rest of Australia. In fact, it's the smallest state. And it only has a population of about half a million people. The capital city is Hobart, and you can see Hobart over here in the southeast of the island. Tasmania is known for its natural beauty, as you can see in these pictures. And thousands of tourists visit every year to see its mountains, lakes, forests and long white beaches. Winters in Tasmania are cold compared to the mainland, and there is a lot of snow, especially in the mountains. And people can ski from July to September. Well, that's about it! I hope one day you'll be able to visit Tasmania and see its beauty for yourself. Thank you.
