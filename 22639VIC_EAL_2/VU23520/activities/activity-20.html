Show transcript – An English school

This is a large school, and we have students from lots of different countries studying here. There are lots of students from Asia, Africa and the Middle East. We also have a few from Europe.

Students come to English class on weekdays. Most students attend class for 10 hours a week, but students can also study online at home. Students can study for a Certificate 1, 2 or 3 in English. In our classes, students study speaking, listening, reading and writing in English and also Computers. Our classes are good for people who are looking for a job or who want to improve their English for other reasons.

There’s a lunchroom where the students can get together and chat while they eat lunch. It has tables and chairs. The lunchroom also has a kitchen where you can heat food to eat and water for hot drinks.

Our school has a computer room for computer classes. We also have a library. You can use the library to study, or you can borrow books and learning resources to help you practice your English.

I’m sure you’ll like it here; it is a very good school. The students are friendly. The teachers are friendly too. They are trained teachers and have many years of experience teaching English.
