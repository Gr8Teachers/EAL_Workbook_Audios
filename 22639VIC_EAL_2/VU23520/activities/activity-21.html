Plan

When you are planning your presentation, using a planning table can help you organise your ideas.

You will give a short presentation (2 to 3 minutes) about a place you know well, so it is important to know what you will speak about. Your presentation can be about:

  • a country like your home country, or Australia

  • a place like your city, town or suburb

  • a favourite place like a park, a building, or a beach.

You will give an introduction then talk about the place. Your presentation will include:

  • an introduction, including your name, and the name of the place

  • information about the place, including:

  • why the place is familiar to you

  • interesting facts about the place

  • your opinion of the place

  • thank the audience.

The table below is an example of a plan for a presentation about Australian food.

Item Details Example
Topic Choose a relevant, familiar topic. Something you know about or have experience with. Australian food
Purpose What is the purpose of your presentation? To inform the audience about different types of Australian food
Audience Who is the audience? Students in my class and my teacher
Greetings How you will greet the audience. Good morning, everyone. How are you all today?
Introduce yourself How you will introduce yourself. My name is Zoe.
Introduce your topic What is the topic of your presentation?

Today, I will be talking about my favourite Australian foods:

  • Australian barbeque

  • Australian seafood

  • Australian vegetables.
Main point What is the main idea of your presentation? I love Australian food because it is unique and delicious.
Supporting details What details support the main idea?

  • At a barbecue, you can find sausages, steaks, and prawns cooked on a grill. It's a great way to spend time with family and friends outdoors.

  • It’s easy to buy really fresh seafood.

  • There are lots of different fresh vegetables in the supermarkets.
Conclusion Summarise your topic. Give your opinion. Australian food is diverse and delicious. Whether it's a barbecue, seafood or delicious fresh vegetables, there is always something tasty to enjoy in Australia
Visual aids used (optional) Make a note of the visual aids you will use. For example, diagrams, photos, PowerPoint presentations, digital stories, personal objects, maps or apps. I will create a PowerPoint with photos of the food I like.

Show transcript – An English school

This is a large school, and we have students from lots of different countries studying here. There are lots of students from Asia, Africa and the Middle East. We also have a few from Europe.

Students come to English class on weekdays. Most students attend class for 10 hours a week, but students can also study online at home. Students can study for a Certificate 1, 2 or 3 in English. In our classes, students study speaking, listening, reading and writing in English and also Computers. Our classes are good for people who are looking for a job or who want to improve their English for other reasons.

There’s a lunchroom where the students can get together and chat while they eat lunch. It has tables and chairs. The lunchroom also has a kitchen where you can heat food to eat and water for hot drinks.

Our school has a computer room for computer classes. We also have a library. You can use the library to study, or you can borrow books and learning resources to help you practice your English.

I’m sure you’ll like it here; it is a very good school. The students are friendly. The teachers are friendly too. They are trained teachers and have many years of experience teaching English.

Show transcript – Tasmania 2

Today I'm going to tell you a little about Tasmania. It is Australia's smallest state. And it's also the only state that's an island. First, let me show you a map of Australia. You can see that Tasmania is right at the bottom of Australia, separated from the mainland by Bass Strait.  Only about 500,000 people live in Tasmania. Hobart is the state capital and the largest city. It's in the southeast of the island. Summer lasts from December to February, and winter is from June to August. Tasmania is near Antarctica, so it's cold for a lot of the year. It snows a lot in winter, and sometimes it snows in summer as well. Tasmania is famous because it's very beautiful and clean. There are a lot of mountains, lakes, forests and long white beaches. A lot of tourists visit Tasmania, especially in summer. That's all I've got time to tell you about Tasmania, but I hope one day you'll have the chance to visit it and see its beauty for yourself. Thank you.
