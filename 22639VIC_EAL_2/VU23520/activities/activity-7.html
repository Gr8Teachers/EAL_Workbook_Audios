Linking words

Linking words are the ‘glue’ that joins your ideas in writing. They make your ideas ‘stick together’ and easier to understand. This is called cohesion. Linking words also gives order to your sentences.

The table below lists some linking words with examples.

Linking words Definition
Firstly, secondly, thirdly, finally

These words are used to sequence ideas. They are usually used at the beginning of the sentence.

Example: Firstly, I’d like to tell you about… Secondly, ….
Then

To indicate how the text is developing

Example: We’ll finish our work then we can leave class for lunch.
By the way

Used to add a new point or idea to a sentence or phrase

Example: By the way, we should go for a walk tomorrow. We haven’t gone for a walk for ages.
Anyway

Used to support a point or idea that was just mentioned

Example: I know you can swim, anyway you should still wear a lifejacket.
So

To indicate cause and effect or result

Example: I saw the coin on the ground, so I picked it up.
After that

To sequence ideas, usually in the middle of a sentence

Example: I saw a huge whale, and after that, I saw the calves.

Show transcript – Terrigal

Let me tell you about one of my favourite places. It’s a small coastal village just north of Sydney. It’s called Terrigal. Terrigal is famous because of its broad, golden, sand beach. And the peaceful Terrigal lagoon is right next to the beach. Drive around Terrigal beach on the main road or walk along it to the other end. You will find beautiful Wamberal lagoon surrounded by a magnificent national park.

And Terrigal is ideal for surfing. Many people go to the beach before sunrise to swim and surf and watch the sun come up.

Along the side of the beach is a vibrant esplanade with cafes and restaurants. The pretty rockpool at the southern end of Terrigal Beach makes it popular with families. As is the boardwalk. You can walk along the boardwalk above the ocean to Terrigal Haven. At the Haven, you can have a fun family picnic and watch the seagulls diving into the ocean to catch fish.

Or you might even spot humpback whales. They migrate south annually in June.

So, for a much-needed break, Terrigal is a great place to go.
