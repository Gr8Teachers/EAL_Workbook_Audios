The 6th largest country in the world, Australia, is over 4 thousand kilometres across, from east to west. It is an ancient land where the Indigenous people have lived for 50 thousand years. The British first came to settle in Australia in 1788, but it was only in 1901 that the Australian nation was established, becoming the Commonwealth of Australia. Australia is a big country, but it only has a population of about 23 million people. The population has more than doubled in the last 50 years, mainly because of immigration. And nearly one quarter of the population was born overseas. Most of Australia's people live along the east and southeast coasts, particularly in the two largest cities, Sydney and Melbourne. Australia is famous for its natural beauty and places such as Uluru and the Great Barrier Reef. It's also known for its unique native animals, including the kangaroo, koala and platypus.
