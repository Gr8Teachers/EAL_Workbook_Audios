Let me tell you about one of my favourite places. It’s a small coastal village just north of Sydney. It’s called Terrigal. Terrigal is famous because of its broad, golden, sand beach. And the peaceful Terrigal lagoon is right next to the beach. Drive around Terrigal beach on the main road or walk along it to the other end. You will find beautiful Wamberal lagoon surrounded by a magnificent national park.

And Terrigal is ideal for surfing. Many people go to the beach before sunrise to swim and surf and watch the sun come up.

Along the side of the beach is a vibrant esplanade with cafes and restaurants. The pretty rockpool at the southern end of Terrigal Beach makes it popular with families. As is the boardwalk. You can walk along the boardwalk above the ocean to Terrigal Haven. At the Haven, you can have a fun family picnic and watch the seagulls diving into the ocean to catch fish.

Or you might even spot humpback whales. They migrate south annually in June.

So, for a much-needed break, Terrigal is a great place to go.
