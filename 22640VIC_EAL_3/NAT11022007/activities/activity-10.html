Rhythm

Rhythm arises from the contrast between stressed and unstressed syllables.

Pace

Pace refers to how fast or slow you speak the words in sentences.

Many speakers of English speak so rapidly that their listener has no time to understand the words let alone the meaning of the sentence. If you speak too rapidly the stressed and unstressed sounds of the words become blurred together which impacts the listener’s understanding.

Linking

It is common in spoken English to join the sounds at the end of one word to the beginning of another. The linking of sounds adds rhythm. It also makes the speech sound smooth and fluent.

You can link sounds between the following:

  • a vowel and a vowel

  • a consonant and a vowel

  • a consonant and a consonant.
