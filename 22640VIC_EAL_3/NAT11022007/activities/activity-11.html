Pausing

Pausing is the strategic use of pauses. Pausing can add emphasis, create suspense, or signal transitions in a conversation or speech. In casual conversations, you can use pauses to go back and correct your work choices.

We use ‘//’ to mark pausing.

Today, I’ll talk about the importance of pausing. Do you use pauses when you are speaking? // I do.

Pausing helps the speaker // emphasise their point.

In formal discussions and presentations, you will need to use pauses more carefully than in casual conversation. A pause can keep your audience interested in what you are about to say. Use pauses when you are about to reveal an interesting perspective. In the pause, look at your audience and do a quick visual check to see if they are keeping up with you. If the audience members are looking puzzled you may need to repeat or restate your information using different words to enable them to keep up.

Show transcript – Job seeking process

Wendy: Good morning, everyone! My name is Wendy, and I'm a college vocational counselor. Today I'm going to talk about preparing yourself for the job seeking process.

Now, is anyone in the class working already? 

Audience: Yeah.

Wendy: OK. Some of you have found a job. What sort of work are you doing?

Man: Weekend, part-time. I'm stacking shelves in a supermaket.

Wendy: OK. And how did you find that job?

Man: My brother-in-law works there.

Wendy: OK, so you used your contacts. One of the most common ways of finding a job. I'll come back to that point later. Now, in this morning's talk, I'll cover a number of issues. Firstly, where job seeking courses are held. And then the idea of networking, followed by barriers to finding a job. And finally, how to overcome these barriers. Oh, by the way, stop me if at any time there's something you don't understand.
