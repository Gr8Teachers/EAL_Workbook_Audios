Show transcript – Clean up Australia

Abdul: Good morning everyone. My name’s Abdul Latif and I’m a Clean Up Australia Day volunteer. I’m very pleased to be here today to talk to you about this event. 

You may or may not have heard about Clean Up Australia. Does anyone know what happens on this day? No? Then this is a good opportunity for me to share with you and to invite you to join us in cleaning up your new home. 

So what is Clean Up Australia? It’s a non-profit organisation founded by Ian Kiernan in 1989.

Ian believes that simple, easy actions can help protect the health of our environment and we can all help to make a difference. So every year on the first Sunday in March thousands of Australians volunteer to clean up our local environment. We collect and remove rubbish. Take me for example, I joined Clean Up Australia last year and I worked with other volunteers to clear up our local creek. You wouldn’t believe what we found there: bottles, plastic bags, wallets, a shopping trolley and even an old fridge! Yep, I’m not kidding. 

Did you know that Australians are among the highest producers of waste, per person, in the world? Shocking, isn’t it? And 80 million plastic bags are dumped in Australia each year. This creates a huge threat to our marine life. So how can you be a part of our clean up community? You, your family or friends can either come along and join us at a designated site or choose a spot in your local area. It could be a park, waterway or bushland and help clean the area up. 

If you want more information, go to our website. It’ll tell you where the existing clean up sites in your area are and you can also register online to be a volunteer or ring us on 1800 282 329. OK. Are there any questions? Yes, the student at the back. 

Student: Ah yeah. Do you provide protective clothing? 

Abdul: Ah... no, we actually don’t, um... so you should bring protective gloves, ah... a hat and sunscreen of course, as it can get pretty hot in March. And it’s best to wear old clothes as you’ll probably get pretty dirty. Any more questions? No? Then I hope to see you on Clean Up Australia Day. 

If you have any more questions, please feel free to see me after this. Thank you very much for your time.
