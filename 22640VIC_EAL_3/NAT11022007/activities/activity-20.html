Show transcript – Discussion on healthy living

Teacher: Good morning Fei! How are you?

Student: Hello Alex. Good thanks.

Teacher: Ok, Well today, we’re going to have a discussion about food, nutrition, health, and wellbeing. Are you ready?  

Student:  Yes, sure. 

Teacher: Great! Let's start with talking about healthy habits. What are some healthy habits you have or that you want to develop?

Student: Well, I try to eat a balanced diet with lots of fruit and vegetables. Um, I bring my lunch to TAFE and... I also drink plenty of water and tea every day.  But right now, I only exercise once a week, so I want to exercise more regularly., I’d like to make it a daily routine.

Teacher: Yes, that sounds like a good idea. Regular exercise is definitely important.  So can I ask ..  What kind of exercises do you do now?

Student: Um usually I go for walks and do some light jogging. I also like doing yoga because it helps me relax and stretch my muscles.

Teacher: Yep, Yoga is excellent for the body and the mind. 

Student: How about you? Can I ask... What exercise do you like to do?

Teacher:  Actually, I do yoga too. I get up early and do a 7am class at the local gym. It helps me feel great all day… and… on the weekends I play badminton. It’s fun and I can catch up with my friends…

Student:  Really?  Badminton? Wow! I used to play badminton. Where do you play?

Teacher:  Oh, just at a local badminton club.

Student: Mmm, that sounds interesting. I’ll see if there’s a club in my area too.

Teacher: Good idea, Well, now,… let's talk about food.  Tell me... what food do you like? And which of your favourites are healthy or unhealthy?

Student: Ah… I love eating Italian food, especially pasta and pizza. 

Teacher:  Mmm… I think it’s best to only eat pizza and pasta occasionally. What do you think?   

Student: Yes, I guess so. They’re not the healthiest of options, I know… but they’re so tasty. I’m so tired after working at night  I just want to have a quick snack.  

Teacher: (EMPATHETIC tone)  Mmm yes, I understand, you’re saying when you’re busy it’s difficult to cook. May people have this problem. And next how about stress. How do you handle stress? 

Student: When I’m stressed after TAFE I go for a walk in the local park and when I’m working I walk in the mornings... It helps me clear my mind and forget about any problems.

Teacher: Yep, that’s a great idea, isnt’ it? I walk my dog in the evenings too. 

Student: Yes, I think it's very important. It helps me to recharge and stay focused. Otherwise, I feel stressed and can’t sleep.

Teacher: Mmm... What other changes would you like to make to improve your health and wellbeing?

Student: I want to eat less take-away food and cook more meals at home. I just need to get organised and plan my meals for the week. Last week I cooked a large batch of soup and froze it in small containers.

Teacher: That’s a fantastic idea! Home-cooked meals are much healthier. Do you enjoy cooking?

Student: Yes, I do when I’ve got time. It’s fun to try new recipes and experiment with different ingredients. Plus, I know exactly what’s going into my food.

Teacher: That’s great. Cooking can be a very rewarding activity. Lastly, do you have any tips or advice for someone trying to develop healthier habits?

Student: I think starting small is important. Make one change at a time and be consistent. Also, find activities and foods that you enjoy, so it doesn’t feel like a chore. And don’t be too hard on yourself—progress takes time.

Teacher: Excellent advice. Thanks for discussing your thoughts and experiences today. I’ve really learnt a lot.
Student: Yes me too. I'll definitely check out the badminton club. Thank you!
