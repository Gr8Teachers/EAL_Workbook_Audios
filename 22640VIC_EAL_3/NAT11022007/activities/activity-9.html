Patterns of word stress

Stress patterns is the way syllables are stressed in a word. It is a combination of stressed and unstressed syllables. We can use capital /0/ and lower case /o/ to show the word stress pattern. The capital /0/ shows where the word stress is located in the word.

Here are some examples:

Word Syllables Pattern
definition de-fi-ni-tion ooOo
analysis a-na-ly-sis oOoo
economic e-co-no-mic ooOo
procedure pro-ce-dure oOo
availability a-vai-la-bi-li-ty oooOoo
beneficial be-ne-fi-cial ooOo
assessment a-ssess-ment oOo
evidence e-vi-dence Ooo
significant sig-ni-fi-cant oOoo
interpretation in-ter-pre-ta-tion oooOo
