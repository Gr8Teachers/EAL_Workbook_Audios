Show transcript – Transaction in a store

Customer: Good morning. I’d like to see someone about this problem I have with my phone. [falling intonation on phone]

Technical Support: No problem. Welcome to the store. How can I help you?

Customer: So, I’ve been having issues with this phone for a while now. [falling intonation] It was repaired quite recently at THIS store because it kept turning itself off. [falling intonation] But now, since I got it fixed, it’s turning off even more frequently. [falling intonation on the word 'frequently']

Technical Support: Hm, okay.

Customer: You know, sometimes it turns off in the middle of calls. It’s really frustrating.

Technical Support: You don’t think you’re accidentally hitting the on/off button on the side, do you? That can sometimes happen.

Customer: Definitely not. I’m very careful with the way I hold it. [stress the word ‘hold’ and use falling intonation]

Technical Support: And it seems to be charging, okay? Are you using the proper charging cable — not one from the 2-dollar shop?

Customer: Yes, it’s all charging fine. As I said, it’s just that it keeps turning off.

Technical Support: Okay, right. Just checking. So I just need to ask you...how long have you had this phone?

Customer: I haven’t had it for very long so I really don’t think this sort of thing should be happening. Can you tell me what’s going on?

Technical Support: Okay, yep. I’ll have a quick look at it and see if I can work out the problem.

Customer: That would be good.

Technical Support: I’m just running a few tests…… [pause while Technical Support looks at phone]

Customer: This phone WASN’T very old, and it has ALREADY been repaired ONCE. What I’d REALLY like is to get a REPLACEMENT or a REFUND.

Technical Support: I see. Just give me a minute to check a few things… So, can you tell me first...what was the problem when you brought it back last time?

Customer: It was exactly the same problem last time. After I got it back from you last time, I had assumed the phone was fixed but it just started switching itself off again.

Technical Support: Okay, it doesn’t look like I can help you, so l’m going to have to send it back to the repairer.

Customer: Look, that’s not going to work for me because I can’t study without my phone. I really just need to get a replacement or a refund.

Technical Support: Well, I’ll have to check the date of purchase first. Also, I’m not sure if the warranty on this model includes a replacement or a refund.

Customer: But I really need my phone for work and TAFE. Can I talk to your manager?

Technical Support: Ah... Okay. It’s coming up on the screen now. You bought the phone three months ago, so it’s still under warranty. Great news! It looks like we CAN give you a replacement phone or a refund.

Customer: So, does that mean I can get a BRAND new phone if I want?

Technical Support: Yes that’s right. Or a refund if that’s better?

Customer: I would like a new phone please. And do you mean I can get that today?

Technical Support: Yes of course. Come over to this counter and I will get the salesperson to help you select a replacement phone.

Customer: Okay, that’s great. Thank you.
