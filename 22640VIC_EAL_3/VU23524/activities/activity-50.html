Clarifying the outcome

The last stage of a phone transaction is important as it ensures everyone understands the goal and ways forward which holds both parties accountable. This approach makes it easier to address needs in a quick manner and achieve successful results.

Here are examples of clarifying an outcome.

Thank you for your help. So, just to clarify, you will be sending a replacement device, correct?I appreciate your assistance. So, to confirm, the refund will be processed back to my original payment method?Thanks for sorting this out. So, to my understanding, the technician will visit my home tomorrow to fix the issue?Thank you for that. From my understanding, you will be escalating this issue to your supervisor for further investigation. Is that right?

Show transcript – Telephone conversation

Eddie: Good morning. This is Eddie your Aus Telnet Customer Service Representative. How can I help you?

Sasha: Good morning Eddie. I’m calling because I’ve been having some serious issues with my home internet.

Eddie: Okay, we can definitely help you. Can I just get some details first?

Sasha: Sure.

Eddie: Great. Can I ask for your full name, date of birth and phone number please?

Sasha: Yes, it’s Sasha Tran. My date of birth is the 14th of April 1988 and the phone number is 0400 508 294.

Eddie: Thank you. And what’s the address of the property where the internet is connected?

Sasha: It’s 56/89 Young Street Canterbury.

Eddie: Okay, got it. So what seems to be the problem?

Sasha: Well, for the past 10 days or so, things have got pretty bad.

Eddie: What exactly has been happening?

Sasha: I keep getting internet outages that last for ages. I mean, the internet just drops out COMPLETELY for hours at a time… And then even when it works, I’m getting REALLY SLOW speeds. It’s INCREDIBLY FRUSTRATING!

Eddie: I'm sorry to hear about the issues you've been facing. Now first I just want to check if you’ve done a simple reboot? Have you turned off the modem and turned it back on again?

Sasha: Yes, I’ve tried that several times. It doesn’t change anything.

Eddie: Okay. Always good to try that first. Let me check your account and see what's going on. [after a brief pause] Yes, I see here that there was a technical fault in the area reported a week ago. Unfortunately, it seems the issue hasn't been resolved as quickly as anticipated.

Sasha: Yes, I would really like the problem fixed as soon as possible.

Eddie: I understand.

Sasha: You see, I’m a student. I need to have internet connection. I haven’t been able to do my online studies from home and I’m falling behind. I also can’t watch my streaming services. Now, I know that’s not as important, but I just need to unwind I’m paying for monthly subscriptions, which I’m not using!

Eddie: I apologise again for the inconvenience caused. I completely understand your frustration, Sasha.

Sasha: Yes and I’m NOT happy paying for an internet service I haven’t had for 10 days. So what I’m saying, is…I don’t want to be charged for the internet that I can’t use this month, and I would also like Aus Telnet to pay for the streaming subscriptions I can’t use.

Eddie: Hmm... Let me escalate this matter to our technical team right away to ensure it's resolved promptly. I'll have someone look into it immediately.

Sasha: Okay, thank you. That would be good. Does that mean you will you have to send someone out to look at it HERE?

Eddie: It depends what the issue is exactly. We may be able to solve the problem without coming to you.

Sasha: Alright.

Eddie: Now, regarding compensation for the inconvenience caused, I'll make a note of your request for a reduction in your bill for the 10 days of poor service.

Sasha: Thank you. I appreciate that. And also compensation for the streaming services?

Eddie: Yes, that’s noted too.

Sasha: Can you give me an estimated time for when the internet service will be back to normal?

Eddie: Well, I'll prioritise your case and get back to you with an update within the next few hours.

Sasha: So let me get this straight… I’ll hear back from you TODAY? And you can tell me if a technician needs to come to my place? And you’ll also be able to tell me how long before it’s fixed, right?

Eddie: Yes that’s right, Sasha. In the meantime, is there anything else I can help you with today?

Sasha: No thanks. I’ll just be waiting for your call.

Eddie: We'll do our best to resolve this issue swiftly. Thank you for your patience and your understanding. Have a great day.

Sasha: You too. Thanks. Bye.
