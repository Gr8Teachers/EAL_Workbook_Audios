Show transcript – Food Preparation

Anna: Well, when it comes to a sensible diet, we can divide food into 4 main groups. First of all there are the grains and we should eat plenty of grains like rice, bread and pasta.
Next, lots of fruit and vegetables. After that comes the protein foods like meat, cheese and other dairy food.
Many Australians eat far too much meat and cheese. Fish is in this group too, but most people don’t eat enough fish. Fish is good for the heart and other things too.
And finally we should eat as little as possible of fatty food, of sugar and salt. We know that people who eat a lot of fat become overweight and develop heart problems.
