Show transcript – Nature or nurture

Liz: Oh. You look tired Vinh.

Vinh: Yeah, I couldn’t sleep last night. I’m stressed out. Daniel, my eldest son’s preparing for his Year 12 exams.

Liz: [laughing] He’s the one doing the exams, not you!

Vinh: [laughing] I know but the whole family’s stressed.

Liz: So what's he want to do?

Vinh: Uh he wants to be a musician.

Liz: O oh!

Vinh: Play in a band, but I want him to go to university.

Liz: Why? There’s nothing wrong with being a musician.

Vinh: Oh yes there is. There’s no future, no job security and look musicians are always broke.

Liz: Oh.

Vinh: I came to Australia for my family to have a better future. I want my kids to get a good education and a university degree in medicine or law.

Liz: Oh.

Vinh: In my culture education is everything!

Liz: Mmm.

Vinh: I want Daniel to be a doctor. He’s smart enough but he’s just not interested.

Liz: Look, my son’s best friend is struggling through a law degree. He hates it but he’s doing it for his parents. He, he really wants to be a primary school teacher but his dad was totally against it and the poor kid is absolutely miserable.

Vinh: Yeah but Daniel has to set a good example for his younger brother.

Liz: Uh.

Vinh: His best subject at school is art. Huh! Can you believe that?

Liz: Well you never know. Your sons might become rich and famous one day.

Vinh: [laughing]

Liz: But I think our aptitude and interests lead us to jobs. In some families, there’re lots of actors.

Vinh: Ah.

Liz: Father, son, grandson.

Vinh: Oh I don’t think it’s in the genes. I think we create our own lives and future. We don’t have to be like our ancestors. I’ll help my children create their futures.

Liz: Really? Well in my opinion children should be free to choose what they want to do in life. It’s their lives, not ours to decide. Do you want your children to be rich or do you want your children to be happy? I strongly believe we should enjoy what we do - jobwise.

Vinh: You know I spend heaps on coaching school to help them do better in school.

Liz: Do they have time for sports or hobbies?

Vinh: They don’t need to play sport. You’ll never convince me that sport is as important as study.

Liz: Oh I don’t know. I just prefer to support my children in their interests. To love them and be there when they need my help. Oh, well .. I’d better get back to work or I won’t have a future. See ya.

Vinh: Yeah see ya. I’d better get back to work too.
