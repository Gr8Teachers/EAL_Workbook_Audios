Show transcript – Housing crisis in Australia

Listen to the discussion

Sam: G’day Zoe. Do you mind if I sit here?   

Zoe: Sure Sam. …How’s work?  

Sam: Yeh, pretty good thanks. How’ve you been lately? 

Zoe: Actually, I’m a bit concerned about the price of housing ….um you see, my cousin’s coming to live here and it’s impossible to find her a place to rent … I think the prices are too expensive, … it’s pretty bad.  

Sam: Mmm, I see. So, what do you think are the reasons?  

Zoe: Well, the way I see it is there’re 2 problems. Firstly, housing prices are too high compared to our wages, so people can’t afford to buy their own place, and… this means, they have to rent and because of the huge demand landlords are charging too much.  

And the second problem is there just aren’t enough rental properties out there, so landlords are leasing sections of their homes. You know…I watched the news last night, and they said landlords were charging too much, like, there was a tiny balcony, in the city, advertised for $360 dollars a week. I think it’s a scam! It’s illegal to rent a balcony as a bedroom.  

Sam: Yeah. It is a scam, but I think landlords should be able to charge whatever they like. 

Zoe: I mean, I reckon the real reason for the housing crisis is negative gearing. I read an article that said wealthy people can get a mortgage from the bank to invest in property. So, they do this, then at tax time they get a tax rebate and pay much less tax. So, I think this is really unfair for young people and new migrants like my cousin. What do you think?  

Sam: Well, I agree one reason for the housing crisis is high interest rates. However, high interest means landlords have to pay back more on their loan. So, why should they have to pay more while renters pay less? And another thing, I think the other reason for the shortage of homes is there just not enough tradies. Do you know, school leavers these days don’t want to learn a trade? They think they should go to uni. But chippies, sparkies and brickies make heaps of money. So, I honestly, feel that the government should do more to encourage new apprenticeships. 

Zoe: Mmm, yeh, I see what you mean but honestly, I don’t think housing should be an investment. Housing is a basic right! Everyone needs a home! 

Sam: Yes, I understand what you’re saying. So, what do you think is the answer? 

Zoe: In my opinion, I think the Aussie government should stop negative gearing. This would bring down the cost of homes and then we can all buy one. 

Sam: Mmm, yes, but on the other hand. We still need more homes built. I think the government should encourage more social housing or other options like tiny homes or even caravans. (laughs) 

Zoe: Ha ha, we can all take to the road and live in caravans, ha ha. 

Sam: Ah, that would be nice…(Sighs).. One day!…well…. back to work. Nice talking to you. 

Zoe: Yes, same here, see you later.

Sam: Bye
